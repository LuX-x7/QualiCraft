About

A research tool that keeps the record.

QualiCraft is built for interview studies where the analysis has to be defensible: every coding traceable to a passage, every model suggestion reviewable, every change to the codebook recoverable. Use a private online account or run it locally. Online projects are saved on the QT server; local projects stay on your machine.

  • Choose where your data lives

    Online accounts use separate project stores on the QT server. Choose local use when your study requires it, and check consent and institutional requirements before uploading.

  • Every suggestion can be traced

    A suggestion is anchored to character offsets, and its quotation has to match the source exactly or it is discarded. Accepting it stays your decision, and the decision is recorded.

  • Keep decisions with the evidence

    Choose TA, qualitative content analysis or binary Comparative QCA. Save method decisions, an Analysis Table and Narrative Analysis in a versioned Research report. Export editable Word, the highlighted source notebook, figures, counts and reproducible code.

  • The interface follows the study

    Switch a capability off and it leaves the interface rather than sitting there greyed out. A content analyst and a grounded theorist should not see the same screen.
